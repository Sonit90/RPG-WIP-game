# The Druid

A healer and a caster in human form, whose second resource is **not spent by filling it**.
Astral Power is generated by casting, and it does nothing at all until the player presses the
button that arms it — after which the next *compatible* spell spends it and is empowered.

```text
every Druid cast ──► +Astral Power (the ability's own column, 5–15)
                             │
                             ▼
                  Astral Power 0 ─────────────── 100
                             │
        press ASTRAL POWER (G) ──► ARMED. Nothing has been spent yet.
                             │
        the next compatible spell ──► spends 100, and is empowered
                             │
        ┌────────────────────┴─────────────────────┐
        ▼                                          ▼
  damage: Moonfire +50 % and for ever        healing: +30 % on every
          Sunfire +30 % and a conversion              healing event the
          Wrath's debuff goes wide                    spell produces
```

**Nothing is consumed automatically.** Reaching a hundred is not a proc: there is no hidden
timer, no free empowerment and no threshold that fires on its own. Arming and then walking
away leaves the Druid armed, which is a decision rather than a leak.

---

## What was reused, and what is new

Nothing in this class is a second copy of a system that existed.

| Mechanic | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| The thirteen abilities | The Druid's rows, `engine-game/src/druid/abilities.rs`, assembled into the one table `ability::ABILITIES` (`engine-game/src/ability/`) |
| Costs, generation, empowerment eligibility | `firemage::DruidRow`, four columns on those rows |
| Cooldowns, charges, the global cooldown | `AbilityBook`, unchanged |
| Casting, queueing, cast bars, interrupts | `sim/firemage_exec.rs`, unchanged |
| Damage | `Simulation::strike` → `Simulation::deal_damage`, the one place health goes down |
| Healing | `GameWorld::heal` and the `Healing` event, the same path the Warrior's self heal uses |
| Damage over time and healing over time | `status.rs`, extended with haste, an indefinite flag and an expiry payout |
| Buffs and debuffs | The same `Status`, on the caster or on the victim |
| Area targeting | `collect_in_shape` for enemies, `collect_friendly_in_shape` for allies |
| Dispel classification | `status::DispelSchool`, asked of the status kind |
| Movement speed and damage reduction | `MovementConfig::max_speed` and `DefenseProfile::damage_reduction`, both existing |
| Combat log and telemetry | `CombatEvent`, with five new resource variants |
| Encounter reset | `GameWorld::restore`, which already clears statuses |

**New:** `engine-game/src/druid/mod.rs` (Mana, Astral Power, the channel and every tuning
constant) and `engine-game/src/sim/druid_exec.rs` (the executor). Both are the same shape as
the Warrior's pair.

## Resources

### Mana

`crate::combat::Resource`, the generic pool that already existed — not a Druid-specific
implementation. Ten thousand points, regenerating a hundred and fifty a second in and out of
combat.

* Every heal costs Mana. Moonfire and Sunfire cost a little.
* **Wrath costs nothing**, which is what makes it the filler.
* A cast with no Mana for it is refused before anything is spent: no charge, no global
  cooldown, and `NOT ENOUGH MANA` on screen.
* Cost is paid when the cast *lands*, which is the same rule the ability book's charges
  follow. A cast that is cancelled therefore costs nothing.

### Astral Power

`druid::AstralPower`. Zero to a hundred, generated per cast by the ability's own
`astral_generation` column, and **never decaying**. Deliberately not `combat::Resource`: that
type regenerates on a clock, and the one fact this resource must never acquire is a rate.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| below 100 | The button is refused: `NOT ENOUGH ASTRAL POWER` |
| 100, not armed | The button can be pressed. The bar says `READY` |
| armed | The next compatible spell is empowered. The bar says `ARMED` and turns pale |

Pressing the button spends **nothing**. The hundred is taken by the spell that consumes the
arming, which is announced as `AstralPowerSpent`.

**Compatible:** Moonfire, Sunfire, Wrath, Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Lifebloom, Wild Growth,
Swiftmend, Nourish, Tranquility.
**Not compatible:** Nature's Cleanse, Wild Rush, and the Astral Power button itself — by the
`astral_empowerable` column rather than by a branch in the executor.

## Stats

The universal ones, through the existing character sheet: **Intellect** (the main stat, and
therefore spell power), Stamina, Critical Strike, Haste and Versatility. No Druid-specific
stat exists and none is needed.

**Haste shortens the gap between the ticks of every Druid damage and healing over time.** It
is applied where the status is ticked, against the stats of *that* step, so a Druid who gains
haste while a Moonfire is running ticks the rest of it faster — the same no-snapshot rule
Ignite already followed. The duration is unchanged; the number of ticks inside it is not.

## The abilities

Every number below is a row of `ABILITIES` or a constant in `druid::tuning`. Nothing in the
executor, the interface or the tests writes one down.

### Moonfire — `1`

* **Purpose:** the instant that starts a fight and keeps working while the Druid heals.
* **Instant**, 30 m, 150 Mana, +12 Astral Power. Nature damage.
* 120 % spell power on impact, then a **18 s** burn ticking 35 % every 2 s, hasted.
* **Astral:** impact **+50 %**, burn **+50 %**, and the burn's **duration becomes
  indefinite** — it is removed by the target dying, by the encounter resetting or by a
  dispel, and by nothing else. It cannot survive a reset, and nothing about it persists
  between encounters.
* **Targeting:** the current enemy, line of sight required.
* **Reset:** the status goes with `GameWorld::restore`, like every other.

### Sunfire — `2`

* **Purpose:** Moonfire for a pack.
* **Instant**, 30 m, 8 m radius around the target, 250 Mana, +12 Astral Power. Nature damage.
* 100 % on impact to everything in the radius, then the **same 18 s burn Moonfire applies**,
  at the same coefficient, on each of them.
* **Astral:** damage **+30 %** on the impact and on the burn, and every enemy hit also takes
  **Astral Sunfire** for **12 s**.
* **Astral Sunfire:** while it is on an enemy, **all damage dealt to that enemy by a party
  member heals every party member for 30 % of the damage dealt**. It is read in
  `Simulation::deal_damage`, once, with the damage that actually landed — so it works for
  single-target and area damage alike, for the whole party's damage rather than the Druid's,
  and for no other source. Healing is not damage and cannot re-enter that path; a re-entrancy
  flag refuses a conversion inside a conversion in any case.

### Wrath — `3`

* **Purpose:** the free filler, and the debuff the whole party's magic is amplified by.
* **1.5 s cast**, 30 m, **0 Mana**, +15 Astral Power. Nature damage, 185 %.
* Each successful cast has a **25 % chance to restore 1 % of maximum Mana**, through the
  class's own seeded random source so a replay restores it on the same casts.
* Applies **Nature's Exposure** to the target: **+2 % Nature and Arcane damage taken per
  stack, to 10 stacks (+20 %)**, 15 s, refreshed by every application. Read in
  `deal_damage`, so it amplifies *any* source's Nature and Arcane damage — not only the
  Druid's — and leaves Physical and Fire alone.
* **Astral:** grants **Astral Wrath** for 15 s. While it is up, every Wrath also applies the
  debuff to every enemy **within 10 m of the primary target**, at the same one stack and the
  same duration, and the primary is never stacked twice by one cast.

### Rejuvenation — `4`

* **Instant**, 40 m, 300 Mana, +8 Astral Power.
* **18 s** healing over time, 45 % every 3 s, hasted.
* **Astral:** all healing it produces **+30 %**.

### Regrowth — `5`

* **1.5 s cast**, 40 m, 600 Mana, +10 Astral Power.
* 300 % immediately, then **12 s** of 30 % every 3 s.
* **Astral:** **+30 %** on the direct heal *and* on every tick.

### Lifebloom — `6`

* **Instant**, 40 m, 350 Mana, +8 Astral Power.
* **15 s** of 25 % a second, and when it **expires naturally** it blooms for 250 %.
* Refreshing it restarts the duration, which delays the bloom — the existing status rule.
* Consumed by Swiftmend or removed by a dispel, it does **not** bloom: the payout is attached
  to `StatusEnd::Expired` and to nothing else.
* **Astral:** **+30 %** on the ticks and on the bloom.

### Wild Growth — `7`

* **Instant**, 40 m, **15 s cooldown**, 900 Mana, +12 Astral Power.
* Applies a **7 s** heal over time, 30 % a second, to the target and to every **injured** ally
  within 15 m of them.
* **Astral:** **+30 %** on every tick of every copy.

### Swiftmend — `8`

* **Instant**, 40 m, **12 s cooldown**, 500 Mana, +10 Astral Power.
* **Consumes one of the Druid's own heals over time** from the target — Rejuvenation,
  Regrowth, Wild Growth or Lifebloom, in that order — and heals for 400 % at once.
* Refused outright, before anything is spent, if the target carries none of them.
* **Astral:** **+30 %**. The consumed effect is still consumed; the empowerment changes the
  size of the heal and nothing else.

### Nourish — `9`

* **2.0 s cast**, 40 m, 450 Mana, +8 Astral Power.
* 280 %, **+10 % per Druid heal over time already on the target, with no maximum**. Four is
  +40 %, six is +60 %, and there is no ceiling to reach.
* **Astral:** **+30 %**, applied *after* the heal-over-time scaling.

### Nature's Cleanse — `0`

* **Instant**, 40 m, **8 s cooldown**, 200 Mana, +5 Astral Power.
* Removes **every harmful effect on a friendly target whose school a dispel can name** —
  Magic and Poison today, Curse and Disease as soon as something applies one. A bleed is not
  dispellable and a buff is not something to be rid of, both asked of
  `StatusKind::dispel_school` and `StatusKind::is_harmful`.
* **Not Astral empowerable.**

### Wild Rush — `F`

* **Instant**, self, **25 s cooldown**, no Mana.
* **6 s** of **+50 % movement speed** and **−30 % damage taken**.
* Both are the existing systems: the speed multiplies `MovementConfig::max_speed` for the
  player's own collided step, and the reduction is `DefenseProfile::damage_reduction`, the
  same field Last Stand uses.
* It is a **status on the player**, so it appears on the frame, is cleared by death and by an
  encounter reset with everything else, and has exactly one truth about whether it is running.
* **Not Astral empowerable.**

### Astral Power — `G`

* **Instant**, self, no Mana, no cooldown, generates nothing.
* Arms the next compatible spell. Refused unless the bar is full and not already armed.
* **Not Astral empowerable** — it is the button.

### Tranquility — `R` (Ultimate)

* **Channelled 6 s**, 120 s cooldown, 1500 Mana, 20 m radius, 40 m range.
* Heals every ally within the radius **once a second**, 120 % a pulse: six pulses, fired
  against the cast's own elapsed time so the count is the same at any frame rate.
* The channel **is** an ordinary cast: moving out of it, cancelling it or dying stops it, with
  no second set of interruption rules.
* **Astral:** **+30 % on every pulse**. The empowerment is taken when the channel *begins*,
  because its pulses are empowered from the first one. It never adds pulses or seconds.

## The one healing rule

> **Astral Empowerment multiplies the healing a spell produces by 1.30.**

One function — `Druid::heal_scale(empowered)` — reads one constant, `ASTRAL_HEAL_BONUS`.
Every heal in the kit goes through it: the direct half of Regrowth and Nourish, the tick of
every heal over time, Lifebloom's bloom, Swiftmend's burst and every pulse of Tranquility. The
multiplier is applied where the heal is *built*, once, so a multi-event ability cannot be
multiplied twice and cannot be missed.

## Reset and encounter rules

Everything the Druid leaves behind is a `Status` or a resource, and both are cleared by the
paths that already existed:

| What | Cleared by |
| --- | --- |
| Moonfire, Sunfire, Astral Sunfire, Nature's Exposure | `GameWorld::restore` on the enemy — a death or an encounter reset |
| Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Lifebloom, Wild Growth | the same, on whoever carries them |
| Astral Wrath, Wild Rush | the player's own statuses, cleared by the reset and by a hero swap |
| Mana, Astral Power, the arming, the channel | `Druid::reset` |
| Cooldowns | `AbilityBook::refill`, unchanged |

**Astral Moonfire is the one indefinite effect in the game, and it is still not permanent.**
It has no expiry; it is removed by the target dying, by the encounter resetting or by a
dispel. Nothing about it survives an encounter.

## Interface

* **Two bars** where the FireMage has Heat and Ultimate Energy: Mana, and Astral Power. The
  Astral bar is recoloured *and* outlined when armed, and prints `ARMED` or `READY` — a
  colour alone is not a state.
* **The arranged player frame** shows the same pair, so the resource is visible wherever the
  player put their frame.
* **Thirteen abilities on ten digits**: the bar holds the first ten, Wild Rush and Astral
  Power sit on the two buttons beside the Ultimate (`F` and `G`), and Tranquility is the
  Ultimate on `R`. Nothing renumbers the keyboard for the other two heroes.
* **The empowered highlight** is the one the Warrior's empowered abilities once used: a compatible
  ability lights up while the bar is armed, so "this press will be empowered" has one visual
  language across the classes.
* **Tooltips** are generated from the same rows and constants the executor reads: the cost,
  what the cast puts into the bar, the healing or damage at the current spell power, and one
  line saying exactly what Astral Power would do to *this* ability.

### Known limitations

* **No icon art.** The Druid's thirteen buttons draw their abbreviated names, which is the
  fallback every ability had before the mage's and the Warrior's icon sets were painted.
* **Sounds are borrowed**, chosen by shape, exactly as the Warrior's are.
* **No Druid VFX.** Nothing new is authored this phase; the effects the events drive are the
  existing ones.
* **No talents.** The talent grid is still the FireMage's.
* **No shapeshifting, and no Feral or Guardian.** In human form the Druid is the same rig as
  the Warrior.

## The main hall's healing dummy

A healer needs something to heal. The training arena — and the dungeon's entrance hall —
stands a **friendly training dummy** beside the player: `EntityKind::HealingDummy`, in the
player's own faction, with a **two million point** health pool, **restored to half** rather
than to full so there is always something to heal and always somewhere for an overheal to go.

* It is **in the party**, so the party frames show it and a party-wide heal reaches it.
* It is **not a combatant**: `TargetQuery::is_combatant` keeps it out of every hostile scan,
  so nothing aggroes onto it and no enemy walks past the player to hit an inert post.
* Every area query in the game asks the factions rather than "not the caster", so no area
  spell can land on it.
* `R` — the reset key — puts it back to half, not to full.

## Talents

Eight talents, bought with talent points (tiers 1-4 cost 3 / 4 / 4 / 5). The rows and every
number live in `assets/catalog/talents.json`; `engine-game/src/talent/druid.rs` is the
behaviour (`DruidTalentEffects`, one field per number the executor reads). A character starts
with 5 points and earns 1 per successful run (+1 at key level 3 or higher).

| Talent (tier, cost) | Effect |
|---|---|
| Swift Wrath (1, 3) | Every 4th consecutive Wrath is instant and generates double Astral Power. Any other spell (not the Astral Power button) breaks the run. |
| Verdant Sunfire (1, 3) | Sunfire radius +50 %, Sunfire tick damage -20 %. |
| Deep Exposure (2, 4) | Nature's Exposure maximum +4 stacks, its duration -30 %. |
| Living Seed (2, 4) | Regrowth and Swiftmend leave a seed worth 30 % of the heal, paid when the target next takes damage within 10 s. Pays under Regrowth's name. |
| Overgrowth (3, 4) | Swiftmend cooldown -3 s; each Swiftmend refunds 10 Astral Power. |
| Astral Attunement (3, 4) | Empowerment costs 75 Astral Power; every empowered bonus (Moonfire, Sunfire, the conversion, the healing bonus) is 15 % weaker. |
| Barkskin Instinct (4, 5) | Wild Rush also gives allies within 8 m 25 % damage reduction for 4 s (the `Barkskin` status). |
| Tranquil Mind (4, 5) | Tranquility cooldown -40 s, Mana cost -50 %, healing -15 %. |

## Damage balance (talent pass)

The Druid is a healer; its damage was too high (Wrath ~66 % of it, Nature's Exposure averaging
~11 stacks). Single-target DPS with no talents went from 239 / 297 / 379 (none / medium /
top gear) to **188 / 232 / 294, about 78 %** (`engine-sim --class druid --iterations 20`). No
healing number was touched. The levers, in the order they were pulled:

* Nature's Exposure **maximum stacks 15 -> 10** and **per stack 3 % -> 2 %** (ceiling
  +45 % -> +20 %): it amplifies every Nature and Arcane hit the Druid deals, so it was the
  biggest single multiplier. Deep Exposure gives four stacks back.
* **Wrath coefficient 220 % -> 185 %**: Wrath is still the largest share, but a third of the
  throughput now comes from the debuff being cheaper rather than from the filler hitting
  softer.
* Empowered Moonfire (+50 %, permanent) is unchanged.

Eight-target throughput fell less (677 -> 620 at top gear) because Sunfire carries it.

## Tests

`crates/engine-game/tests/druid.rs`, 35 tests against a real arena, plus the unit tests in
`druid/mod.rs`, `status.rs` and the two interface modules. They pin, among others: the
eighteen-second durations; Astral Moonfire's +50 % on both halves, its indefiniteness, and its
death with the target and with the encounter; Sunfire's area application and its conversion,
including that the conversion runs exactly once per party member per hit; Nature's Exposure's
three per cent a stack to fifteen, its refresh, and that it amplifies damage the Druid did not
deal while leaving Fire and Physical alone; Astral Wrath's ten-metre spread with no double
stack on the primary; **+30 % on every healing spell and no more**; Nourish at zero, one and
four heals over time with no cap; the dispel removing Poison and Magic and leaving a bleed and
a buff; Wild Rush's speed, its mitigation of a real incoming hit, and its expiry; haste
changing the tick count of a burn; and a reset clearing every one of them.
