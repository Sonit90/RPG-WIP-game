# Economy simulation

Generated by `engine-sim --economy-sim` (regenerate after editing anything in `assets/catalog`). The model, the player's policy and the key curve are documented at the top of `crates/engine-sim/src/economy_sim.rs`.

600 players per class, 900 runs each, seed 0xf12e5eed. A run is 30 ordinary kills, 6 elites, the boss and its chest (see `engine-sim/src/economy_sim.rs`). Key levels: +1 per two runs up to key 20, then +1 per 5 runs up to key 24 (run 39 is the first at key 20, run 59 the first at the cap). Every figure below is a mean over the players unless it says median.

Dust never gates sharpening: owning the pieces does, and past the safe levels the break chance. It also has no other use (Steward's Ember, 20 dust each, is only asked for by the unsafe levels).

| Class | Sockets | Gold to max every gem | Gold to reforge 13 pieces to 90+ | Total | Runs of income |

| Mage | 14 | 16306 | 37154 | 53460 | 438 |

Gem gold is the chain's expected price per max-level gem (1165 gold, from the success chances alone) times the sockets; reforge gold is the expected attempts to land 90+ (13.0 % per attempt) times the price of one at +4 for every one of the 13 pieces, which overstates it (a drop often arrives above the goal). The player also pays 150 gold per maxed gem moved to a better piece.
