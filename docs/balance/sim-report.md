# Balance simulator report

Generated by `engine-sim`. Every number comes from the game's own `Simulation`: the real ability table, cooldowns, damage function and stat pipeline, driven headless by one rotation AI per class. A run is deterministic: the same seed and iteration count reproduce this file byte for byte.

* Seed `4046348013`, 20 iterations per cell, 216 cells.
* Targets are passive dummies with 5000000 health each, so nothing dies and DPS is sustained output. They wander a few metres every two seconds, as in the FireMage study, so cones, lines and melee reach are tested.
* Scenarios:
  * **Single target, 60 s** - `st60`: 1 target(s), 60 s.
  * **Single target, 180 s** - `st180`: 1 target(s), 180 s.
  * **AoE, 3 targets, 60 s** - `aoe3`: 3 target(s), 60 s.
  * **AoE, 8 targets, 60 s** - `aoe8`: 8 target(s), 60 s.

## 4. Rotations

Each AI is a priority list over ability presses and cooldown readiness; it takes at most one action per tick and none during a cast or the global cooldown. The lists below are printed from the code that implements them. They answer, per class: what to cast, when to use cooldowns, and which buffs and debuffs to keep up.

### Warrior / damage on cooldown

1. Last Stand when ready, only with Unyielding: its Battle Fervor stacks are worth the global cooldown.
2. Colossus Strike first while the target is under Executioner's threshold, only with Executioner (the Warrior also picks the weakest enemy in reach).
3. Shield Charge on cooldown (15 s): the biggest hit, and it closes the distance. With Relentless Charge's second charge it yields to a ready Colossus Strike.
4. Colossus Strike on cooldown (8 s): area smash and a bleed scaled from maximum Health.
5. War Stomp on cooldown (4 s): the cleave, stacking Sundered on everything it hits.
6. Shield Bash on cooldown (15 s): pressed as damage; there is nothing to interrupt.
7. Shield Block when ready, only with Bulwark Rhythm: the cooldown it cuts is then a cooldown in use.
8. Recuperate as the filler: no cooldown, and it stacks the Warrior's self heal.
9. Other defensive cooldowns (Puncture Blood, Adrenaline, Battle Trance, Spell Reflect) and Taunt are not part of a damage rotation.

### Druid / damage over time and Wrath

1. Astral Power: press the button as soon as the bar holds 100 and nothing is armed (arming spends nothing; the next compatible spell does).
2. Moonfire: when missing or at 3 s or less. When armed, cast it unless the burn is already permanent (empowered Moonfire: +50% damage, never expires).
3. Sunfire: when anything within its 8 m radius of the most central enemy is missing it or at 3 s or less (9 s when armed).
4. Wrath: the free filler; stacks Nature's Exposure on the target (empowered, on everything around it).
5. Healing spells, Wild Rush and Tranquility are not part of a damage rotation.

### FireMage / Haste (Detonation)

Played by: Default (HASTE / HASTE MASTERY / 0 PTS / 45% HASTE 20% CRIT). Talents: NONE.

1. Moving Fire Beam on a full Ultimate bar, aimed at the pack and kept on it for the whole channel.
2. Fire Mastery on cooldown, once there is a burn (2 stacks) or 30 Heat to amplify.
3. Detonate a full burn when it would kill the target anyway, is about to expire, or is fresh (80% of its duration left).
4. Flame Cone / Firestorm when two or more enemies stand in the pack.
5. Flame Lance while the burn is below maximum stacks.
6. Meteor, then Pyroblast, from 3 Ignite stacks.
7. Flame Lance with a spare charge, then Firebolt as the filler.

### FireMage / Crit (Ignite maintenance)

Played by: Build B (CRIT / CRIT MASTERY / 18 PTS / 20% HASTE 45% CRIT). Talents: METEORIC IMPACT + SPELLFIRE FEEDBACK + BURNING PRECISION + INFERNAL CAPACITY + EXTENDED FLAME.

1. Moving Fire Beam on a full Ultimate bar, aimed at the pack and kept on it for the whole channel.
2. Fire Mastery on cooldown, once there is a burn (2 stacks) or 30 Heat to amplify.
3. Detonate only when holding the burn is worthless (lethal anyway, or about to expire).
4. At a full burn: Pyroblast, then Meteor.
5. Below a full burn: Flame Lance to rebuild it.
6. Flame Cone / Firestorm for a pack, then the payoff spells, then Flame Lance and Firebolt.

**Cooldowns**: every damaging cooldown is pressed the moment it is ready. The Warrior has no offensive cooldown beyond its damage abilities (Last Stand and Shield Block are pressed on cooldown only by builds whose talents make them matter); the Druid has none at all (its Astral Power button is a resource, not a timer); the Mage's only burst cooldown is Fire Mastery.

**Buffs and debuffs maintained**: Mage - Ignite on the primary target, the Fire Mastery window, Heat in the Inferno State. Warrior - Sundered (War Stomp), Bleeding (Colossus Strike), Recuperating. Druid - Moonfire and Sunfire on the target(s), Nature's Exposure from Wrath, and every empowerment spent.

## 6. Talent validation

Is any talent mandatory, or worth nothing? For every class and gear tier (the headline profile of each of none, medium and high) the `Default`, `Build A` and `Build B` builds are measured, then every talent **alone on `Default`** (*solo*) and every talent of the **best build removed from it** (*removal*, "best" being the higher single-target DPS of A and B at that tier).

* **Single target** is a boss sized to die after about 60 s of the class's `Default` build at that gear, so talents that pay on a dying target (Executioner) are seen; DPS is damage that landed over the time to kill. **AoE 8** is the matrix's eight non-dying targets.
* **Solo** = `Default + talent` over `Default`. **Removal** = best build over best build without the talent (so +20% means the build loses a sixth of its damage without it). Costs are not considered: the numbers say what a talent does, not whether it is worth its points.
* **mandatory-ish**: removal costs more than 15% in some fight and tier. **no damage value**: the talent never adds 1% DPS, alone or in the best build, in any fight or tier - not a verdict on a defensive or utility talent, so the **survival proxy** (average share of a physical hit not taken: avoidance, block, armour, reductions, damage-taken effects, read off the player's own defences because the dummies never attack) is shown beside it. Healing and other utility effects are invisible to both numbers.

## Caveats

* These are *damage* rotations. The Warrior is a tank and the Druid a healer; their defensive and healing kits are not exercised: the dummies never attack, so Block, Parry and Dodge never fire, and the Warrior's Valor comes only from its own casts.
* Gear is whatever the catalog's pieces grant, so Versatility, Spirit, Stamina and Armor ride along with the main stat, Haste and Critical Strike; Versatility and Spirit are not chosen for.
* Every class has `Default` (no talents), `Build A` and `Build B`, which buy talents from `assets/catalog/talents.json` within the 20-point budget.
* Colossus Strike's bleed lands on at most three targets (the selected one, then the nearest): that cap is what keeps the Warrior's AoE 8 DPS level with the Mage's. Its direct hit still cleaves everything in reach.
* Targets wander on a seeded timer, so area abilities lose some hits and the Warrior spends some time walking; this is part of the number, not noise.
* Cooldown *max casts* ignores cooldown-reduction effects, so an ability that is reset by another can exceed 100%.
* The Mage starts every fight with a full Ultimate bar and uses Moving Fire Beam at the pull. The bar refills in about ninety seconds, level with the beam's own ninety-second cooldown, so a sixty-second window holds one beam where a long fight holds one every ninety seconds: the 60 s Mage figures overstate the sustained contribution by about half.
