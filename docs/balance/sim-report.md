# Balance simulator report

Generated by `engine-sim`. Every number comes from the game's own `Simulation`: the real ability table, cooldowns, damage function and stat pipeline, driven headless by one rotation AI per class. A run is deterministic: the same seed and iteration count reproduce this file byte for byte.

* Seed `4046348013`, 20 iterations per cell, 168 cells.
* Targets are passive dummies with 5000000 health each, so nothing dies and DPS is sustained output. They wander a few metres every two seconds, as in the FireMage study, so cones, lines and melee reach are tested.
* Scenarios:
  * **Single target, 60 s** - `st60`: 1 target(s), 60 s.
  * **Single target, 180 s** - `st180`: 1 target(s), 180 s.
  * **AoE, 3 targets, 60 s** - `aoe3`: 3 target(s), 60 s.
  * **AoE, 8 targets, 60 s** - `aoe8`: 8 target(s), 60 s.

## 4. Rotations

Each AI is a priority list over ability presses and cooldown readiness; it takes at most one action per tick and none during a cast or the global cooldown. The lists below are printed from the code that implements them. They answer, per class: what to cast, when to use cooldowns, and which buffs and debuffs to keep up.

### Warrior / damage on cooldown

1. Shield Charge on cooldown (15 s): the biggest hit, and it closes the distance.
2. Colossus Strike on cooldown (8 s): area smash and a bleed scaled from maximum Health.
3. War Stomp on cooldown (4 s): the cleave, stacking Sundered on everything it hits.
4. Shield Bash on cooldown (15 s): pressed as damage; there is nothing to interrupt.
5. Recuperate as the filler: no cooldown, and it stacks the Warrior's self heal.
6. Defensive cooldowns (Shield Block, Puncture Blood, Adrenaline, Battle Trance, Spell Reflect, Last Stand) and Taunt are not part of a damage rotation.

### Druid / damage over time and Wrath

1. Astral Power: press the button as soon as the bar holds 100 and nothing is armed (arming spends nothing; the next compatible spell does).
2. Moonfire: when missing or at 3 s or less. When armed, cast it unless the burn is already permanent (empowered Moonfire: +50% damage, never expires).
3. Sunfire: when anything within its 8 m radius of the most central enemy is missing it or at 3 s or less (9 s when armed).
4. Wrath: the free filler; stacks Nature's Exposure on the target (empowered, on everything around it).
5. Healing spells, Wild Rush and Tranquility are not part of a damage rotation.

### FireMage / Haste (Detonation)

Played by: Default (HASTE / HASTE MASTERY / 0 PTS / 45% HASTE 20% CRIT). Talents: NONE.

1. Fire Mastery on cooldown, once there is a burn (2 stacks) or 30 Heat to amplify.
2. Detonate a full burn when it would kill the target anyway, is about to expire, or is fresh (80% of its duration left).
3. Flame Cone / Firestorm when two or more enemies stand in the pack.
4. Flame Lance while the burn is below maximum stacks.
5. Meteor, then Pyroblast, from 3 Ignite stacks.
6. Flame Lance with a spare charge, then Firebolt as the filler.

### FireMage / Crit (Ignite maintenance)

Played by: Build B (CRIT / CRIT MASTERY / 18 PTS / 20% HASTE 45% CRIT). Talents: METEORIC IMPACT + SPELLFIRE FEEDBACK + BURNING PRECISION + INFERNAL CAPACITY + EXTENDED FLAME.

1. Fire Mastery on cooldown, once there is a burn (2 stacks) or 30 Heat to amplify.
2. Detonate only when holding the burn is worthless (lethal anyway, or about to expire).
3. At a full burn: Pyroblast, then Meteor.
4. Below a full burn: Flame Lance to rebuild it.
5. Flame Cone / Firestorm for a pack, then the payoff spells, then Flame Lance and Firebolt.

**Cooldowns**: every damaging cooldown is pressed the moment it is ready. The Warrior has no offensive cooldown beyond its damage abilities; the Druid has none at all (its Astral Power button is a resource, not a timer); the Mage's only burst cooldown is Fire Mastery.

**Buffs and debuffs maintained**: Mage - Ignite on the primary target, the Fire Mastery window, Heat in the Inferno State. Warrior - Sundered (War Stomp), Bleeding (Colossus Strike), Recuperating. Druid - Moonfire and Sunfire on the target(s), Nature's Exposure from Wrath, and every empowerment spent.

## 5. Gear profiles

Gear is a stat block in the game's own rating units, applied through the same path equipment takes, so the rating curve (`x / (x + 100)`) and the main-stat bridge are the game's. It is derived from the item catalog: 13 equipment slots, and at the current cap one source of stats - the boss-loot pool, Uncommon pieces of item level 5 granting +2 rating on each of two stats. A piece is therefore two *stat slots*; the profile says how many pieces are worn and how their 2 x pieces slots are spent. Top gear fills all 13 slots with best-available stats at a x1.10 quality ceiling (the game has no quality scaling yet, so this is the assumed headroom of a best-rolled piece); medium fills 7 slots at catalog quality, four ways.

## Caveats

* These are *damage* rotations. The Warrior is a tank and the Druid a healer; their defensive and healing kits are not exercised, and the Warrior's defence-driven resource is never generated because the dummies do not attack.
* Versatility and Spirit are not part of any gear profile.
* The Warrior has no talents yet (`Default` only). The Druid's `Build A` (damage) and `Build B` (area) buy talents from `assets/catalog/talents.json`.
* Targets wander on a seeded timer, so area abilities lose some hits and the Warrior spends some time walking; this is part of the number, not noise.
* Cooldown *max casts* ignores cooldown-reduction effects, so an ability that is reset by another can exceed 100%.
