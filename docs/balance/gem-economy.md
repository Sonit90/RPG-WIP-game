# Gem economy

Generated by `engine-sim --gem-sim` (seed 0xf12e5eed, 2000 players per key level). Regenerate after editing `assets/catalog/gems.json`.

One player farms a dungeon of 30 ordinary kills, 6 elite kills, 1 boss and 1 chest per run and upgrades every gem of one kind (Ruby) through the chain, spending 50 % of the run's gold income (122 gold per run, from `loot.json`) on it. Drops use `gems.json` through `engine_game::loot::roll_gem`; the upgrade is `consume` 2 of one level, then success at the step's chance, and a failure keeps one. A max-level gem is level 5.

Chain, from the chances alone: one max-level gem is worth 42.3 level-1 gems and 1165 gold of upgrades.
